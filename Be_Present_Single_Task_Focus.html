Be Present: Single Task Focus

Need help focusing right now?

Choose one tiny action, practice coming back when your attention moves, and leave with useful progress.

Other ways to practice

You do not need perfect focus. Notice when your attention moves, then practice returning.

Learn why returning helps

Being present does not make a problem disappear. It creates enough space to notice what is happening now, return to one activity, and choose a useful next step.

Practice statement: I do not have to follow every thought. I can bring my focus back to this moment and take one useful step.

What is the smallest action you can do?

Make it visible and specific—something you can begin without making another decision.

Choose one simple activity.

Pick one activity you can practice for 1-5 minutes. Say to yourself: "For the next few minutes, I am only doing this."

Expect your focus to wander.

Your focus will probably drift. That is normal. You do not need to argue with the thought or push it away.
