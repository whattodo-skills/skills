Express Yourself Clearly

Prepare a clear message for a conversation that matters.

Say what you mean—clearly and respectfully.

Use this practice when you need to share a perspective, make a request, say no, set an expectation, ask a question, clarify a misunderstanding, or try to reach an agreement.

Planning and rehearsal: about 10–15 minutes. Have the real conversation separately when appropriate. Review takes about 2–3 minutes.

Desired outcome: Describe what happened without blame, express your reaction directly, and communicate a specific request, position, question, refusal, or expectation.
