Coping Skill

Ask for Support

Recognize when help would be useful and make one clear request.

Some problems are easier to handle with support

Support may mean having someone listen, helping with a practical task, sharing useful information, or connecting you with professional or community resources.

This practice will help you decide what kind of help you need and turn it into one clear request.

Asking does not guarantee that someone can help. It gives them a fair chance to understand the request and answer honestly.

This skill is for making ordinary requests for emotional, practical, professional, or community support. When there is immediate danger, a medical emergency, or someone cannot remain safe, use appropriate emergency or crisis assistance rather than relying only on this exercise or an informal support person.

Plan for more than one answer

A clear request may receive a yes, no, later, partial offer, request for clarification, or no response. Your next step should not depend on one person giving the exact answer you hoped for.
