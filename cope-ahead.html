Prepare for the difficult moment

Worry imagines what might go wrong. Cope Ahead practices what you will do next.

Choose one upcoming situation, identify the most likely difficult moment, and rehearse a simple response before it happens.

The goal is not to feel perfectly calm or guarantee a good outcome. The goal is to make your next useful action easier to remember.
