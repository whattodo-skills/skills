A setback is an event, not an identity

Mistakes, rejection, disappointing results, missed goals, criticism, and unexpected obstacles can quickly turn into conclusions such as “I always fail” or “I am not good enough.”

This practice helps you describe what happened, decide what still needs attention, and choose a useful next step without using shame as motivation.

Start with one setback that feels manageable enough to review right now.
