Notice

Describe what could have been seen or heard without assigning a motive.

Examples: They are overreacting. They are being unreasonable. They are trying to control me. They do not care. They are making a big issue out of nothing.

Instead of: “They were trying to manipulate me.”
Try: “They repeated the request three times, spoke more loudly, and appeared upset when I declined.”
