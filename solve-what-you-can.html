Examples

Step 3: Sort the situation

Solve what you control. Influence what may respond. Accept what your current actions cannot change.

Acceptance does not mean approving of the situation or giving up on what can still be changed. It means ending unproductive effort against a fact that is not currently changeable.

I can control

My actions, choices, communication, preparation, or use of time.

I may be able to influence

Other people or circumstances that may respond to my actions.

I cannot control

Facts, past events, final decisions, or other people’s reactions.

Step 4: Generate, choose, and start

Option ideas

Gather information · Ask a clarifying question · Make a direct request · Set or restate a boundary · Break the task into smaller actions · Adjust a schedule · Ask for practical help · Correct or repair a mistake · Negotiate · Remove an obstacle · Create a reminder or system · Try a different approach
